On Being Someone's Constant

Some things don't change: a year of talking, a steady flow of Amazon packages, and the absolute certainty that I'm not going anywhere.

Listen, I need to tell you something, and I'm going to dress it up in Times New Roman and call it an article because apparently that's easier than just saying it directly.

We marked our first anniversary on the nineteenth—exactly one year since we spoke for the very first time. I wanted to give you this then, but work and life and the logistics of the week got in the way, so you are reading this on the twenty-first. In a way, though, that delay is the entire point.

Honestly, looking back to that day on the nineteenth of May last year, I am still incredibly grateful that you ever took the chance to reply to a total dork like me in the first place.

The No Back Door Policy

Some people are constantly looking for an exit before they even walk into a room. They keep one foot near the threshold, ready to slip out the back door the moment things get slightly heavy or complicated. It's a defense mechanism, a way to make sure they're never the ones left holding the pieces.

I want you to know, with absolute certainty, that I'm never leaving out the back door. I don't do back doors.

I am here for the long haul. Not just in the "I'll be here if things stay easy" conditional way. I'm here regardless of whether things are smooth or messy, whether we're laughing or trying to figure out a tough day. You don't have to keep one eye on the exit, because I've locked it and thrown away the key.

Working for Us

I'm actually working right now as I write this, and the truth is, I absolutely love it. But there is a very specific reason why I love putting in this effort, and why I have this drive in the first place. Every single thing I'm doing is because of you. You are the reason behind all of it.

"I'm building, planning, and working for you—for us. You're the context for everything I want to achieve."

It makes the long hours feel light, and it gives every task a real purpose. Knowing that we are building a future together turns the standard, boring routine of work into something that feels genuinely exciting.

The Things We Send

I should probably address the fact that I love sending you books via Amazon. It is my favorite way of sending you things I think you'll love. I might not be a big reader myself, but I love picking out books that I know will speak to you, hitting order, and waiting for that delivery notification to hit your phone. It's my way of sending a small piece of a world, hoping you'll enjoy it.

And let's be real about who you are. You're kind in a way that actually matters, and you are incredibly smart. But you're also amazing, cute, pretty, sexy, and hot—and yes, I will state that clearly in black and white text because it's the absolute truth and you deserve to see it written down.

The Resilient Swimmer

You have this quiet strength inside you that you don't always give yourself credit for. You are deeply resilient. No matter what obstacles get in your way, no matter how heavy the tide feels, you will always swim through. You've proven that time and time again.

But the most important part of that equation is that you don't have to navigate the water alone. If the current gets heavy, if the waves get rough, I'm right there beside you. We will always swim together.

The Real Point

What I'm trying to say is simple: you have someone who's staying. Not conditionally. Not temporarily. You have me. Permanently.

I'm here. I'm staying here. That's not changing.

And if you need a little more convincing, I put some updated puzzles below. They are completely blank right now—they will only solve themselves when you're ready to click the buttons and see what's hidden inside.

Always.

—Niv
